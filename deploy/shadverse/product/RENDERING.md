The portrait samples the anatomical contours of the MakeHuman hm08 head. Its 44,478 point sites encode position, smooth normal and ambient visibility. A colour-masked pass over 8,968 triangles sets depth only. The second pass draws all visible colour as individual light-emitting particles. Violet fills the front, magenta sculpts the cheeks and brow, and a cooler rim separates the far temple from the purple environment. A small subset of particles disperses beyond the head and the neckline fades.

## Dependencies, assets and reproduction

- The build uses quantized buffers and GLSL in the existing WebGL2 renderer. The packed prefix gives each quality tier a representative sample. Microphone input and the audio sample share the existing Web Audio analyser.
- The MakeHuman asset names Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier and identifies the CC0 dedication. The build copies the license into the distribution and includes it in the portable HTML. [Pinned hm08 geometry](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj) · [CC0 dedication](web/identity/MakeHuman-CC0.txt)
- To regenerate both binaries, download that revision of `base.obj` and run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python art-tools/sample-face.py /path/to/base.obj` with NumPy and SciPy installed. The sampler checks the input SHA-256. It intersects horizontal planes with the head, interpolates vertex normals, then estimates ambient visibility from 24 depth projections.
- SHADVERSE authored the shaders, environment and glass normal map. The instrument separates the luminous trace from its refractive outer panel and displays the median, peak and time span of the measured sample window.

- The prerecorded English test sentence was synthesized offline using FFmpeg’s Flite source and the CMU `kal` voice. Only the generated waveform is bundled, encoded as MP3 for wider browser playback support. No voice model or synthesis executable ships with the app. [Flite source and license](https://github.com/festvox/flite)
- The existing Vazirmatn and Space Grotesk fonts are self-hosted with their OFL notices. OpenClaw retains its MIT attribution. The preview embeds the font notices. The visual research links above are references only.

## Runtime boundaries

The renderer interpolates states without replacing the canvas. In listening mode, actual input amplitude and frequency pull particles inward and backward. In sample playback they move outward and forward. Manual listening/speaking pose selection is labeled as a demonstration. The microphone is disconnected from the output, and its tracks stop on explicit stop, state change, page navigation, tab hiding and sign-out. The sample ends automatically. Live model output and speech recognition require later integration.

The quality controller caps pixel density and draw frequency, reduces point count after sustained frame delays, and selects a lower tier on software rasterizers. Reduced motion holds a static pose. Hidden tabs suspend drawing, and loss or absence of WebGL switches to a Canvas 2D projection of the same anatomical samples. The mobile performance report distinguishes browser callbacks from actual renderer submissions. Hardware frame presentation, battery draw and physical-device thermals are unverified.

Workspace records are scoped to page memory and are cleared on sign-out. The account directory is served by the existing authenticated Creator endpoint. Browser instrumentation reports the current tab. Production memory, subscriptions, model routing and deployment remain separate from this visual review.
